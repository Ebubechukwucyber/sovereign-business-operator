Payment network

AI operates the business. The chain verifies the money.

A hard trust boundary between intelligence and settlement.

Sovereign uses AI for intake, pricing assistance and proposal generation. Payment confirmation is handled separately through deterministic on-chain verification rules.

The AI never confirms payment. A transaction must satisfy the chain verification rules before it can be treated as confirmed.
